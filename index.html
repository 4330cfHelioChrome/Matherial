Matheriel

A constructed language built entirely from mathematical and logical symbols.

About the Project

Matheriel explores a novel form of communication by replacing traditional alphabets and words with mathematical notation, operators, and relational symbols. It aims to achieve absolute logical clarity and universal structural expression.

Core Philosophy

Instead of relying on spoken phonetics and cultural idioms, Matheriel relies on pure relations, quantities, and operational flows to convey thoughts, concepts, and states.
